### Probes used (so the table below can be read)
- **Cue fired**: `dumpsys audio` keeps a timestamped "playback activity" log; every `SoundPool.play()` of the app's pool (piid 135/143/663, `USAGE_GAME`) writes `player piid:N event:started`. A cue that the gate blocks writes nothing. `qa_helpers.py events` extracts it.
- **Sound stopped**: `dumpsys media.audio_flinger` → "N Tracks of which M are active", polled every ~40 ms.
- **Haptic**: `dumpsys vibrator_manager` "Recent vibrations" names the constant: `constant=6` = `CONTEXT_CLICK` (jump), `constant=17` = `REJECT` (wrong tap).
- **Spoken**: each TalkBack utterance is a new `com.google.android.tts` AudioTrack in the same log (start and stop timestamps give its length), plus TalkBack's "Display speech output" overlay in screenshots.
- **Visual**: `screenrecord` at 12 Mbit/s; frames extracted with their presentation times; `shake_measure.py` gives the horizontal centroid of a cell's drawing per frame (A = 0.10 × 130.29 px = 13.0 px), `ring_measure.py` the held ring's vertical extent, `board_diff.py` which frames change the board and where, `mark_contrast.py` the mark's WCAG contrast against the slab.

### Method notes the table relies on
- **TalkBack and injected input.** With TalkBack on, `adb shell input tap` on the board canvas reached the ViewModel one tap per `input tap` (the first exploratory tap at 23:39:58.722 already produced a wrong tap); a pair sent as a double-tap reached it as two clicks 16–62 ms apart. On Views (the permission dialog, the TalkBack switch) the same injected taps behaved as a real finger: single tap = focus, double-tap = activate, or not at all (the switch needed Tab + DPAD_CENTER). So the announcement contract was exercised by wrong taps that genuinely reached the ViewModel while TalkBack ran, but not through a real user's explore-then-double-tap on the canvas, which the spec itself records as not yet possible (no per-cell semantics, Q-023). Injected `input swipe` and Alt+Right / Alt+Shift+Right were not recognised as TalkBack gestures or shortcuts, so traversal was checked with Tab (input focus, which TalkBack follows and speaks). A real-finger swipe traversal belongs to the phone procedure below.
- **Recording rate.** `screenrecord` on this emulator writes only changed frames; during continuous animation it ran at 33–60 fps (frames 16–17 ms apart in `wt18-light-w2`), under load it dropped frames (the W2 window of `wt18-light-vol0` had a 330 ms gap, so W2 was re-recorded). Durations above are frame-pair differences, ± one frame.
- **Clocks.** Device-side timestamps (`dumpsys`) and host-side ones (`date` around `adb` calls) differ by about 0.3 s; comparisons are made within one clock where it matters.
