## Verdict: FAIL

**F-1 — High. The amendment exempts the six three-button windows from the bound, and its own method says three of them bite — one by more than the Pixel_9.**
Where the exemption is written: D-03q part 1 (1487–1488, "Where the bound does not bite — every three-button phone window measured, and 360×640dp — nothing changes from D-03p"); §4.2.7 "The title bound" part 2 (4089–4090, "every three-button phone window §14 measures, the design baseline, and every window shorter than ≈ 800dp"); §14's loop-1 header (7463–7464, "on the six three-button windows H₀ = ⅘ S₀ and no figure moves"); 15y part 1 (7479, "⅘ S₀ on every window here") and part 4 (7487–7488, "the footer is a fifth of S₀ in both"); 15s's pointer (7362–7363); **15ac part 1** (7591, "on the six three-button windows H₀ = ⅘ S₀ (±0.5dp)"); §13's D-03q row (6783, "On the six three-button windows the figures are unchanged, so an assertion written for ⅘ S₀ goes red only on the two gesture-bar windows"); candy-backdrop §8.1's pointer for §8.4 (1879, "360×800dp — the headroom ≈ 90dp"; "412×915dp — the headroom ≈ 240–330dp with no save").

Consequence: 15ac part 1 requires both "the row's bottom at or above the bound" and "on the six three-button windows H₀ = ⅘ S₀"; on three windows they cannot both hold, so the criterion cannot pass as written. 15y parts 1 and 4 and 15s's pointer assert the same coincidence; §13's row tells the developer to keep the ⅘ S₀ assertions on those windows and add only the gesture-bar ones. ## The ruling on its merits
- **Arithmetic.** The corrected table reproduces to the dp (Checks). The bound is stated in the backdrop's own frame and read from `TITLE_REACH` rather than copied, so it cannot drift from BD-10. Taking `entrance_slide_distance` off it is the right subtraction: the cascade draws a band 16dp below its layout position (§10), and the deepest entrance frame still clears the crest-minus-bob line by 14.5dp on the Pixel_9 and 8.8dp at 360×640dp. The row only ever moves up from H₀ when a chip arrives and back down to H₀ when one leaves, so no state and no frame of the room's motion crosses it.
- **Q-033 and the gaps.** The founder chose "above"; the bound keeps the block above the first element's floor and sends the capped excess below the tiles, where it is a larger footer and not a hole: every gap between consecutive elements is still the band gap (15y part 3, 15ac part 3 (d)). That is the only placement consistent with Q-004, 15p and Q-033 at once, and D-03q says so.
- **The floor.** Right in law and precedent (F-3 is about saying it plainly, not about the ruling).
- **360×640dp.** The column's total is unchanged (headroom + footer = S₀ in every state), so 15k, its fold and its ≤ 120dp overflow are untouched; QA's window bites by 12.0dp and still seats tile 1 at 340.8 against the 334 midpoint; the three-button window does not bite and keeps its 6.4dp shortfall (F-6 for the margin).
- **Thumb zone (15g).** Every tile top is below its midpoint in every chip state on every window computed here, biting or not; the footer is 24–30% of S₀ on the biting windows (F-2 for the fourth clause).
- **Chip-arrival motion.** Unchanged in shape: the room still comes from the headroom, H₀ in place of ⅘ S₀; the row and the ribbon ride up together, monotonically, over `ENTRANCE_MS`; with animations off the next frame is settled; the tiles hold still wherever R ≤ H₀ (true on every window computed here at default scale).
- **Testability.** 15ac parts 1–2 are JVM-readable on supplied insets with the window's H; part 3 (a)–(d) are device reads with a method, a matrix (fresh install, one save, both; light and dark; Pixel_9 and 360×640dp) and pass lines, and they reach the dark no-chip state the first check asked for. The one defect is F-1: as written, part 1 cannot pass on three of its windows.
