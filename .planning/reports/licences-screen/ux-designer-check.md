## Findings

**F-01 — High — `docs/ux/licences-screen.md:744` (LS-AC15), also `:478-484` (§4B).** The Compose pass line `onNodeWithText("End of text").performScrollTo()` cannot pass on the §4B body. The body is a `LazyColumn` (LS-08's exception), so the "End of text" item is not composed until it scrolls into view, and `onNodeWithText` fails with "no node found" before `performScrollTo()` runs. `performScrollTo()` works on the §4 `Column` body (LS-AC05), not on a lazy one. Fix: on the lazy body the test scrolls the **list** node — `onNode(hasScrollToNodeAction() and <the list's tag or key>).performScrollToNode(hasText("End of text"))`, or `performScrollToIndex(paragraphCount)` — and then asserts the node is displayed. Same wording in §8.1 LS-AC10's "Google notice text (load a fixture text, scroll to the end …)".

**F-02 — High — `:544-546` (§5.1 "One stop per thing"), `:506-509` (§5 Labels and roles), `:136-138` (LS-06), `:243-249` (§3.1).** The contract says every entry "is one merged node (`mergeDescendants = true` through `clickable`, as every `SettingsLinkRow` is)". An inert entry has no `clickable` (LS-06: "no click action"), so nothing merges it: its title, credit, licence and address `Text`s are three or four separate TalkBack stops. `SettingsScreen.kt` confirms the inherited gap — `SettingsLinkRow` with `onClick = null` has no merge, and the file contains no `mergeDescendants` or `clearAndSetSemantics` (locator result). As written, the four inert entries break pass line 1 ("every stop in the table's order and nothing else"), pass line 3, and the stop count at `:552-553`. Fix: state that an inert entry's card carries `Modifier.semantics(mergeDescendants = true) {}` with no role and no action, and that the merge is a property of every entry card, tappable or not; add it to LS-AC06's Compose assertions (one node per inert entry, no `OnClick`, no `Role`).

**F-04 — Medium — `:742` (LS-AC13), `:550-554` (§5.1 "Lists are collections"), `:441` (§4A, the intro as the first item).** The QA oracle — "the last row's TalkBack position announcement names the same total as the shipped `index.tsv`'s line count" — rests on "as Compose gives it".
