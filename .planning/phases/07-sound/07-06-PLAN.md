<objective>
Close the two things that cannot be proven JVM-side and one that can only be proven against a real
build: that the resource shrinker keeps every sound in a minified release APK, that the game never
ducks the player's own audio, and that the curated set actually sounds like candy clay.

Purpose: RESEARCH assumption A3 is the phase's highest-consequence unknown — `isMinifyEnabled` and
`isShrinkResources` are both on for release, and the failure mode is "every sound works in debug,
every sound is silent in release", discovered at the worst possible time. SOUND-06's review phrase
(*start a podcast, play a full level — the podcast never dips*) is enforced by platform audio
policy and cannot be faithfully asserted in Robolectric. And D-04 deliberately deferred all
aesthetic approval to exactly this moment.

Output: a `## Release shrink verification` section appended to `docs/audio/CREDITS.md`, and a
signed-off manual audit.

  <action>
Build the minified, resource-shrunk release APK: `./gradlew :app:assembleRelease --offline`.

Locate the pinned `aapt2` binary in the Gradle cache (the same `aapt2-9.1.0-*` build the project
already uses; find it under `~/.gradle/caches/` rather than assuming a path) and run
`aapt2 dump resources` over the produced release APK. Filter for `raw/sfx_`.

All eleven `sfx_*` raw resources must be present. `isShrinkResources = true` removes resources with
no static reference, and every cue is referenced as a compile-time `R.raw.*` constant from
`SoundCue`, so retention is expected — but expected is not verified, and this is exactly the check
the repo already performs for `font_certs.xml`.

If any sound IS stripped, do not paper over it with `resources.getIdentifier()` (that would defeat
the shrinker entirely and reintroduce magic strings). Diagnose why the static reference was not
traced and report the finding before changing anything.

Append a `## Release shrink verification` section to `docs/audio/CREDITS.md` recording: the date,
the exact Gradle and aapt2 commands run, the APK path, the count of `raw/sfx_` entries found, and
the verdict. Keep it to a short block in the document's existing voice — this is evidence, not a
new document.
  </action>

**1. First tap is never silent (SOUND-01).**
Force-stop the app so the next launch is cold. Launch it, go straight into a level, and tap a peg
as fast as you can. The selection sound must be audible on that very first tap. Repeat twice more
from a cold start.

**2. The podcast test (SOUND-06) — the review phrase.**
Start a podcast or music in another app at a comfortable volume. Without pausing it, switch to Peg
Solitaire and play a full level through to the Results screen — jumps, an undo, a hint, an invalid
tap, the win, the star pops, the buttons. Confirm:
  - Your podcast never dips, fades or pauses, not even momentarily at the win.
  - The game sounds ride the **media** volume slider: pressing volume-down changes both together.
  - Nothing stops when you background the game.

**3. Immediate silence (SOUND-05).**
While rapid-tapping pegs so several sounds are overlapping, open Settings and switch sound OFF.
Sound must cut out immediately, including anything mid-playback — not fade out on the next tap.
Switch it back ON: you should hear exactly one soft confirmation pop. Now leave Settings and
re-open it: opening Settings must be silent. Toggle haptics on and off: silent.

**4. The aesthetic audit (D-02, D-03, D-04) — this is the decision D-04 deferred to now.**
Play at least one Classic level and one Challenge level and listen for:
  - Does the set read as **soft candy-clay pops** — squishy, rounded, playful? Not wooden, not
    sterile UI blips.
  - Does it sound like **one family**, or does any single sound stick out as borrowed?
  - Is the invalid tap noticeably quieter and non-punishing?
  - Does the removal cue layer *under* the jump rather than competing with it?
  - Is the win louder but not startling?
  - Build a same-peg chain of 2-3 jumps: does the rising pitch feel like a reward? Undo one — does
    the pitch step back down?
  - On Results: do the star pops land with the stars? Does a 3-star finish resolve on a note a
    2-star finish never reaches?
  - Name any duds. They will be swapped from the CC0 sources before the phase closes.

**5. D-10 confirmation (one check, expected to be a no-op).**

Results button. You should hear our click ONCE, not a doubled click. Compose emits no platform tick,
so this is expected to pass with no code behind it — confirm rather than assume.
