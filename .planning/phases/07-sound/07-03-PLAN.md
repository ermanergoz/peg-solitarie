<objective>
Give the board its voice. Derive the same-peg run index from the move history that already exists,
add the three monotonic ordinals for the cues that currently leave no state trace, and fire every
in-game cue from a `LaunchedEffect` keyed on those values — the established `JumpHaptic` idiom.

Purpose: this is where SOUND-01 and SOUND-02 are actually delivered, plus SOUND-03's first beat
(D-14: the win sound fires as the winning move lands on the board, not on the Results screen).
The pitch ladder is the personality of the phase, and deriving it from `moveHistory` rather than
storing it is what makes D-08 ("undo steps the ladder back down one") free and impossible to
desynchronise.

Output: `runIndex` on `MoveTransition`, three ordinals on `GameUiState`, their increments in
`GameViewModel`, six small cue composables in `GameScreen`, and two test files.
</objective>
