## Phase Goal

**As a** casual puzzle player, **I want to** play a peg solitaire board end-to-end — tap a peg, see its legal destinations, tap a hole to jump with animation, watch the jumped peg removed, reach game-over, and restart — **so that** I can enjoy the core game loop on a foundation that proves the full stack works.

> Faithful user-story restatement of the ROADMAP Phase-1 goal (which is in outcome-prose form). Role sourced from PROJECT.md ("for casual puzzle players on Android"). No scope invented. This is the Walking Skeleton entry plan; Plans 01-01 → 01-05 together deliver the slice.

<objective>
Stand up the entire Gradle/Kotlin/Compose build scaffold and the CI standards gates **before any feature code**, so the standards criteria (QUAL-01/02/03) are green from the very first commit and can never be retrofitted. Two-module split (`:domain` pure-JVM + `:app` Android), a `build-logic/` convention-plugin layer, the version catalog, detekt + ktlint + ArchUnit domain-purity test + a custom no-dp/sp-literal gate, and a gitflow-gated CI workflow — all passing on near-empty placeholder code.

Purpose: The pure-Kotlin domain is the project's #1-risk core; making its purity and the code-standards gates structural and unbypassable up front is a locked project decision (PROJECT.md, RESEARCH Q1/Q4/Q6 Commit 0).
Output: A buildable two-module project whose `./gradlew ktlintCheck detekt check assembleDebug` is green with no game logic yet.
</objective>
