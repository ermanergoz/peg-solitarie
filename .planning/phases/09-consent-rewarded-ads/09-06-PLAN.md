<objective>
Invert the two assist entry points so nothing is ever charged for an assist the player does not
receive (ADS-06), and add the four `GameUiState` fields the opt-in dialog and the snackbar need.

Purpose: `onHintRequested()` today requests the grant **before** it runs the solver, and `onUndo()`
requests it **before** the empty-history guard. Both orders are harmless while the gateway auto-grants
for free, and both become real charges the moment plan 09-08 adds an allowance and plan 09-10 adds a
real ad. An allowance unit could be refunded; a watched 30-second ad cannot. Fix the order first,
prove it, and let the funnel land on correct ground.
Output: four new `GameUiState` fields carried at all four board-install sites, both entry points
reordered, the found hint stashed, and a dedicated ordering test class.
</objective>
