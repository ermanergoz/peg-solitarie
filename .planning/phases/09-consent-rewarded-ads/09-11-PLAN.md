<objective>
Verify on a real device everything that cannot be asserted in the JVM suite, and record the results as
committed evidence: the UMP consent flow (CONS-01, CONS-02), the two Settings privacy surfaces
(CONS-03, REL-05), a real test rewarded ad end to end (ADS-05), and the clock freeze across a full ad
watch (ADSF-01).

Purpose: UMP has no fakeable seam and renders nothing without a server-published consent message, so
these behaviours are structurally manual. Their absence is also **invisible from a non-EEA test
location** — which is exactly why this plan gates on the AdMob console before it gates on the device.
Output: an itemised `09-DEVICE-EVIDENCE.md` with per-item results, plus a final green full-gate run.
</objective>

    dialog (09-06/09-08), the dialog and snackbar on the gameplay screen (09-03/09-09), the two
    Settings privacy rows (09-07), the clock freeze (09-04), and the real GMA Next-Gen rewarded
    gateway now bound in `PlatformModule` (09-10).
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Install a **debug** build on a device or emulator (`./gradlew :app:installDebug`). Debug builds
    resolve Google's test ad unit and enable `ConsentDebugSettings` with `DEBUG_GEOGRAPHY_EEA`, so an
    EEA consent form is reachable from any location. Emulators are AdMob test devices by default.

    Work through each item and record pass/fail **per item** — a single global "looks good" is exactly
    what the Phase 7 retrospective flagged as insufficient:

    1. **CONS-01 — fresh install, consent first.** Clear app data, launch. The consent form appears
       **before** the Home screen and before any ad could have been requested.
    2. **CONS-01 — refreshed every launch.** Force-stop and relaunch: the app does not re-prompt a
       consented user, but consent info is refreshed (no crash, ads still work). Then invoke
       `consentInformation.reset()` via the debug path and relaunch — the form appears again, replaying
       a first install.
    3. **CONS-02 — no ad before consent.** While the form is on screen, no ad request has occurred and
       no ad UI is visible anywhere.
    4. **CONS-04 / D-12 — decline consent.** Decline, then play. Spend past the free allowance:
       assists remain free, **no opt-in dialog ever appears, and no snackbar appears**. This is v1.0
       parity, and seeing *any* ad-related UI here is a failure.
    5. **CONS-03 — Settings, consent region.** In EEA debug geography, Settings shows both a "Privacy
       policy" row and a "Manage privacy choices" row. Tapping the latter opens the UMP privacy-options
       form in-app.
    6. **CONS-03 — Settings, outside a consent region.** With debug geography disabled (or on a
       non-consent-region configuration), only the "Privacy policy" row appears.
    7. **REL-05 — the policy opens.** Tap "Privacy policy": the live URL opens in the system browser
       and the page loads.
    8. **ADS-01/ADS-02 — allowance then offer.** Take three hints: all free, no dialog. The fourth
       raises the themed dialog naming the count and the reward. "No thanks" leaves the board, the
       timer and the hint state exactly as they were.
    9. **ADS-05 — reward only when earned.** Accept and watch a Google **test** rewarded ad to
       completion: the hint is revealed and hints are free again. Then exhaust again, accept, and
       **dismiss the ad early**: nothing is granted, nothing is refilled, and the game is unchanged.
    10. **ADS-04 — the rescue banner.** Reach a "no winning move" position with undos exhausted: the
        banner's undo action opens the **same** dialog. With undos remaining, it undoes instantly and
        for free. The banner itself shows no ad affordance.
    11. **ADS-06 — a truthful "no winning move" is free.** From a dead position with the allowance
        exhausted, request a hint: the rescue banner appears and **no dialog and no ad** occur.
    12. **ADSF-01 — the clock is frozen.** Note the elapsed timer, watch a full test rewarded ad, and
        note it again on dismiss: the reading must be the same (±1s from whole-second truncation, in
        the player's favour). Then confirm it resumes and keeps counting.
    13. **ADS-03 — fail-open.** Put the device in airplane mode, exhaust the allowance, accept the
        offer: after the bounded ~5s wait the assist lands anyway and a snackbar reads "Ad unavailable
        — hints refilled for free". No error wall, no dead end.
    14. **Invalid-traffic guard.** After all of the above, the AdMob dashboard shows **zero**
        impressions from this device. Debug builds must be serving test units only (Phase 6 D-05).
  </how-to-verify>
