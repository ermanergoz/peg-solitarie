**Two scoping constraints that must not be lost:**
- **Saved Games stays off this interface.** `GamesService.kt:13-16` already says so ("Cloud save (Saved Games) is explicitly out of scope here — it arrives as a second data source behind `ProgressRepository`, not as a method on this seam"). That sentence is load-bearing for Phase 13 and must survive the extension verbatim.
- **PGS v2 has no sign-out and no sign-in wall.** Do not design a `signOut()` or any "sign-in required" gate — v1 patterns do not port.

**Why `showLeaderboard`/`showAchievements` take no Activity and return no result:** PGS v2's `getLeaderboardIntent()` / `getAchievementsIntent()` return `Task<Intent>`; the Phase-11 implementation will resolve the Activity from `ForegroundActivityProvider` (plan 06-05) and call `startActivity`. Threading an `ActivityResultLauncher` down from the Route is unnecessary — v2 returns nothing the app must act on. `suspend` on those two costs nothing today and is what lets Phase 11 `awaitActivity()` with a timeout. `unlockAchievement`/`incrementAchievement` stay non-suspend to match the existing fire-and-forget `submitScore` precedent.

**Sealed-type note:** `GamesSignInState` is (with `RewardOutcome` from plan 06-01) one of the first two sealed types in this repo. `GameStatus` is an `enum class` — copy its member-by-member KDoc register, not its structure.
</context>
