<objective>
Build the pure-Kotlin achievement kernel in `:domain`: the per-solve telemetry carrier
(`SolveTelemetry`), the 20-value `AchievementId` roster, the `AchievementThresholds` constants
object, the SQL-shaped `ProgressAggregate` value type (plus the `ClassicBoard` domain enum), and
the single `AchievementRules.evaluate` pass that turns an aggregate into unlocks + absolute steps.

plan builds against — 12-05 carries `SolveTelemetry`, 12-06 fills `ProgressAggregate` from SQL,
12-07 feeds it to `AchievementRules` and maps `AchievementId` to a Play Games ID string, 12-08
attaches names/descriptions/points to the enum in the Play Console checklist. Landing the
contracts first is what stops the downstream plans from re-deriving them.

Output: five new pure-Kotlin source files and three JVM test classes. Zero Android imports, zero
Play Games ID strings, zero SQL, zero SDK. No dependency, no Gradle change.
