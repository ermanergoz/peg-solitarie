<objective>
Make the three skill facts of PGSA-02 observable for a single play: how many hints the player
took, how many undos they took, and whether the last peg landed on the board's target hole. Count
them in `GameViewModel` on the assist funnel's success path, persist them in the resume snapshot
so process death cannot launder an assist away, compute `lastPegOnTarget` from the finishing
board, and carry all three to Results as defaulted, clamped nav args that `ResultsViewModel`
resolves into one `SolveTelemetry`.

Purpose: without this, three of the twenty achievements (Hint-free, Undo-free, Center finish) are
unearnable, and the `play_session` table plan 12-01 creates has nothing truthful to store. This is
the producer half of D-08's telemetry chain; plan 12-06 is the consumer half.

Output: two new nullable snapshot fields, one new finish-event payload type, three new `Results`
route args, one `SolveTelemetry` on `ResultsViewModel`, and a JVM spec proving the counting rules.
No new dependency, no schema/SQL change, no `ProgressRepository` signature change.
</objective>
