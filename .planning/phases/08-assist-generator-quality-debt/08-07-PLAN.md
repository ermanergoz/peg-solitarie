<objective>
Re-anchor Diamond-41's star bands on its proven floor, and make sure a resumed game keeps its board's
target.

D-03 puts per-board star thresholds in this phase; D-04 says Diamond's bands mirror the global shape,
re-anchored on F: `F` gives 3 stars, `F+1..F+2` gives 2, worse gives 1. RESEARCH's Pattern 5 makes
this one formula rather than a per-board table — with `minimumPegs = 1` the derived bands are
3 stars at `<= 1` and 2 stars at `<= 3`, which is byte-identical to today's global bands. Diamond's
`minimumPegs = 2` yields 3 stars at `<= 2` and 2 stars at `<= 4`. The player-visible change is small;
the truthfulness change is large.

The second half of this plan is Pitfall 3, which RESEARCH flags as invisible in normal testing:
`SnapshotMapper.toBoardSpecOrNull` reconstructs a `BoardSpec` structurally from the persisted blob
rather than re-resolving it from the catalog. With a defaulted `minimumPegs`, every RESUMED
Diamond-41 game would silently revert to a one-peg target — the hint would instantly say "no winning
move" again and stars would be computed against the wrong bands. RESEARCH's warning sign for this
defect is literally "`SnapshotMapperTest` untouched by the plan while `BoardSpec` gains a field".

This plan depends on 08-06 because D-05 forbids anchoring a star band on F until F is proven. Do not
start it before 08-06's replay is green.

Purpose: make the score a player sees on Diamond-41 agree with what the board can actually do.
Output: derived per-board bands, a threaded call site, and a fail-closed resume path.

Note (closing RESEARCH assumption A6, already verified): `LevelResultEntity` stores
`mode, levelId, stars, bestTimeMs, bestScore, completedAt` — `pegsRemaining` is NOT persisted, so no
one-shot re-derivation of stored Diamond stars is possible. Old Diamond bests upgrade through the
existing upsert-on-better path on next play, exactly as CONTEXT assumed. Do NOT write a Room
migration for this.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
