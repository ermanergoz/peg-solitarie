<objective>
Close DEBT-02: prove Diamond-41's fewest-pegs floor is exactly 2, by committed line and by invariant.

**This plan is the A1 gate for the whole phase.** RESEARCH's F = 2 finding came from the
researcher's own scratchpad harness, not from this project's `RulesEngine`. Nothing may anchor on
F = 2 until a line has been regenerated and replayed through the real, unchanged `RulesEngine` and
observed to terminate at exactly 2 pegs. Plan 08-07 (star bands) depends on this plan for exactly
that reason.

The proof has two halves, and RESEARCH made both cheap:
- **Reachability** (D-05a): one committed golden line, generated by beam search under
  `UPDATE_GOLDEN=true` and replayed step-by-step through `RulesEngine.legalMoves` in CI. The existing
  `AuthoringSolver`/`RecordedSolutions`/`SolutionTest` pipeline already does this for four boards.
- **Optimality** (D-05b): F - 1 = 1, and one peg is precisely what the GF(4) colour invariant
  excludes from the centre start. Verified exhaustively: 0 of 41 single-peg positions satisfy it.
  This needs no new search and no new pruning — it was the half everyone expected to be infeasible.

The prior "floor is ~5 pegs" claim in three in-code KDocs came from 50,000 random rollouts, which
plateau at 5 because rollouts are far too weak a search to find a floor. A width-400,000
mobility-ordered beam search reaches 2.

Purpose: make F a fact, so Diamond's star bands and its hint target rest on a proof rather than on a
best-known result.
Output: `BeamSearchAuthoring`, a committed `diamond41.txt`, a target-aware `SolutionTest`, and
`DiamondFloorOptimalityTest`.

**Escalation clause (D-05).** If the beam search cannot reach 2 pegs, or the committed line does not
replay clean to exactly 2 through the unchanged `RulesEngine`, STOP. Do not lower the assertion, do
not edit `BoardConstants.DIAMOND41_FEWEST_PEGS_FLOOR`, and do not proceed to plan 08-07. Report the
observed terminal peg count and escalate to the user — F reverts to unknown and D-05's escalation
clause fires.
</objective>
