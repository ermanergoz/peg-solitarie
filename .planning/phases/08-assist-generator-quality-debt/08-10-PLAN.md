<objective>
Wire the book into the hint pipeline and prove DEBT-01's universal claim.

D-08 states the guarantee as a universal claim bounded by depth, not a vibe: EVERY position reachable
within the first K moves of European-37, Asymmetric-39 and Wiegleb-45 must resolve within the
deadline, verified by an exhaustive symmetry-reduced sweep rather than a sampled corpus. With the
book in place that sweep is a lookup plus a legality check rather than a solver race, which is what
makes it affordable to assert at all — RESEARCH measured the pre-book K=4 sweep at roughly six
minutes across four boards, dominated by deadline-burning positions.

Two correctness rules from RESEARCH govern the wiring:
- **A book miss falls through to the solver; a book hit recording "no winning move" is
  authoritative.** Do not collapse them at the same point in the chain.
- **Never return a stored move unchecked.** The existing recorded-line branch already re-validates
  against `RulesEngine.legalMoves` (threat T-04-CT, documented in the class KDoc as "Blind
  `solution[moveCount]` indexing is FORBIDDEN"). The book gets the same guard, through the same code
  path, so there is one check rather than two that can drift.

Nothing above `:domain` changes. `GameUiState.noWinningMove`, the rescue banner, the `solverThinking`
flag and `GameViewModel.onHintRequested`'s 1.5s deadline are untouched — CONTEXT is explicit that
this phase adds no UI, and the ViewModel already builds `shouldCancel` from
`SolverBudget.DEFAULT.deadline` and enforces single-in-flight via `hintJob?.cancel()`.

Purpose: turn the committed book into the answer a player actually receives, and prove the claim.
Output: a book-aware `GetHintUseCase`, its behaviour spec, and a gated `DepthSweepTest`.
</objective>

| authoritative "no winning move" → rescue banner | Treating a miss as authoritative would show the banner on a position that is genuinely winnable — the exact defect DEBT-01 exists to remove. |
