<objective>
Rewrite the solver's inner loop on the precomputed tables and fix the restart-policy defect, so the
large Classic boards resolve inside the fixed 1.5s deadline instead of folding to "no winning move".

Two measured defects are being fixed, not one:

1. **Constant factor.** The shipped loop allocates on every node: a `List<Move>` of data-class
   objects, a `sortedByDescending` with a fresh `Random.nextDouble()` per move, and a `Board` copy
   per move. Measured ~0.27M nodes/s versus ~15M for the tuned reference — a ~55x gap that is pure
   allocation, not algorithm.
2. **Restart policy.** `searchWithRestarts` hands every attempt the whole 40M-node `budget.maxNodes`,
   so in production the 1.5s deadline always fires inside attempt 0 and `MAX_ATTEMPTS = 40` is
   unreachable dead code. Peg-solitaire DFS runtime is heavy-tailed — the class KDoc says so — and
   the textbook response is many short restarts, not one long one. Measured: European-37's start goes
   from 172ms (one unbounded attempt) to 33ms (two attempts at a 100k cap).

D-07 is honoured literally: `DEFAULT_DEADLINE` stays 1.5 seconds, and there is no per-board deadline.

Purpose: this is the "stronger solver" half of DEBT-01. It genuinely fixes European-37 and
Asymmetric-39. It does NOT fix Wiegleb-45 — RESEARCH measured 0 of 76 depth-≤4 Wiegleb-45 positions
resolving in 1.5s even at 15M nodes/s, a ~4-order-of-magnitude gap. The opening book (plans 08-08
through 08-10) is what closes that, per the user's explicit approval of "opening book + stronger
solver". Do not attempt to close Wiegleb-45 here and do not narrow DEBT-01's scope.
Output: a bitboard `Solver`, a per-attempt cap, and re-anchored budget contracts.
</objective>
