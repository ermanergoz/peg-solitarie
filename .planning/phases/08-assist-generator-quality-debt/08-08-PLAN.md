<objective>
Generate and commit the depth-4 opening book — the mechanism that turns DEBT-01 from a timing gamble
into a lookup guarantee.

This is the user's explicitly approved remedy. RESEARCH measured that the strengthened solver from
plan 08-05 genuinely fixes European-37 (172ms to 33ms) and Asymmetric-39, but resolves **0 of 76**
Wiegleb-45 depth-≤4 positions inside the 1.5s deadline even at ~15M nodes/s — including depth 0,
which is the shipped start whose one-peg line is already a committed fixture. The gap is roughly four
orders of magnitude; no plausible constant-factor or pruning improvement closes it. The user chose
"opening book + stronger solver" and directed that DEBT-01's scope not be narrowed.

The key insight from RESEARCH §Don't Hand-Roll: every remaining hard problem in this phase is
offline-shiftable. The player-facing deadline is 1.5 seconds; the authoring budget is unbounded. A
book entry is generated with unbounded compute and verified by replaying a complete winning line
through `RulesEngine`, so a book hit is correct by construction and the deadline is irrelevant to it.

Sizing is measured, not guessed: the depth-≤4 frontier is 51 / 312 / 233 / 107 / 76 canonical
positions for English-33 / European-37 / Asymmetric-39 / Diamond-41 / Wiegleb-45. Even stored raw
(un-canonicalised, so no symmetry-inverse move mapping is needed) that is roughly 5,000 entries and
~100 KB of text. K = 4 with raw masks is the locked choice: provably complete over the frontier,
no symmetry machinery, cheap.

Diamond-41 is included even though DEBT-01 names only the three large cross boards. It costs
~850 raw entries and it is what makes D-01's Diamond hint work and what mitigates Pitfall 1 — after
plan 08-04 generalised `Pagoda`, an off-book Diamond hint launches a real 1.5s search, so the book
must be the primary Diamond path, not a nicety.

Also included: every prefix state of each board's committed golden winning line. That delivers the
CONTEXT "Classic recorded-line fast path" discretion item structurally — as book entries keyed by
board mask — instead of by adding on-the-recorded-line tracking to `GameViewModel`, which Classic
does not have today.

Purpose: precompute the answers the runtime cannot reach.
Output: `OpeningBookFormat`, `OpeningBookAuthoring`, and five committed book resources.
</objective>
