<objective>
Put the cadence decision and the ad presentation behind the two forward-moving win actions on
Results, and make navigation wait for the ad.

Purpose: this is where every locked decision in the phase meets: D-14's eligibility predicate,
D-06's action identity, D-12's preload point, D-10's zero-latency fast path, D-08's quiescence
pause, D-09's ad-first-then-navigate, D-11's silent skip, and D-15's shared fullscreen cooldown.
RESEARCH names the structural obstacle: `ResultsViewModel` injects no gateway and owns no
navigation timing at all today, and `ResultsActions` invokes the nav lambdas synchronously inside
`onClick`. Navigation timing moves into the ViewModel here; plan 10-05 rewires the route to it.

Output: two new `ResultsUiState` fields and a `ResultsNavTarget` type, the ViewModel's eligibility
+ sequencing, the D-15 rewarded stamp on `AdMobAdGateway`, a shared `FakeAdGateway`, and the
seventeen behaviours below pinned as tests.
</objective>
