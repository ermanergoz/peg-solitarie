Replace the deliberate `showInterstitial()` no-op with a real Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen
interstitial behind the `AdGateway` seam, by cloning the shipped Phase-9 rewarded path.

Purpose: Phase 9 already paid the cost of learning this SDK — main-thread confinement,
consent gating, foreground-Activity capture before `show()`, single-show discipline,
callback-to-coroutine bridging, fail-open degradation, and the Hilt/ArchUnit SDK-containment
trap. RESEARCH names the dominant risk of this phase plainly: not that anything is hard, but
that a second, subtly different ad-holding class gets written and drifts from the first.
**Symmetry with `RewardedAdSlot` is a design requirement here, not a stylistic preference.**

seam, the real implementation, the stub's opposite-polarity parity, and every existing
`AdGateway` test fake updated to the new seam.
