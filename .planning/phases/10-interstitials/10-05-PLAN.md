<objective>
Render the INT-04 quiescence scrim and move navigation timing out of the button callbacks.

Purpose: `ResultsRoute` currently hands the navigation lambdas straight through to
`ResultsActions`, which invokes them synchronously inside `onClick` — RESEARCH Pitfall 5 names
this as the reason there is nowhere to insert an ad. This plan closes that: the two ad-eligible
taps go to the ViewModel built in 10-04, and the route consumes a one-shot navigation target from
state. The scrim is the INT-04 mechanism itself — a stray second tap must land on the dim, never
on the ad, and a queued back press must not escape either.

Deliberately minimal: `ResultsScreen`'s and `ResultsActions`' parameter lists do NOT change, so
the six existing `ResultsScreen(...)` call sites across the test suite keep compiling and the
"avoid structural Results rework that would prejudge the Stuck-finishes question" constraint in
10-CONTEXT § Deferred Ideas is honoured.

Output: `AdQuiescenceScrim`, its render in `ResultsScreen`, the rewired `ResultsRoute`, one string
resource, and the screen tests that prove the block.
</objective>

| navigation effect -> nav graph | A dropped or duplicated navigation target changes the player's position in the Challenge ladder. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-04 | Repudiation | accidental interstitial clicks counted as invalid traffic | mitigate | `AdQuiescenceScrim` consumes every pointer event at `PointerEventPass.Initial` and swallows back presses, covering the whole screen including the `TopAppBar` (rendered outside the `Scaffold`, not inside its content padding). Proven by the paired swallow/control screen tests. |
| T-10-17 | Tampering | double or lost navigation across the ad | mitigate | The target is a `StateFlow` field consumed by a `LaunchedEffect` keyed on it and cleared through `onNavigationHandled()`; a `Channel`/`SharedFlow` emission could be dropped or re-delivered while the host is STOPPED behind the ad (RESEARCH Pitfall 6). |
| T-10-19 | Elevation of privilege | a non-win action acquiring an ad | mitigate | Structural, not conditional: Replay, Home, Retry and top-bar back never enter the ViewModel, so no code path exists by which they could reach the cadence policy (D-06, INT-03). | T-10-09 | Information disclosure | Results UI | mitigate | No logging call in the scrim or the route (ASVS V7). |
| T-10-SC | Tampering | dependency installs | mitigate | This plan adds NO dependency. |
