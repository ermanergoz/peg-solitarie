<objective>
Close the phase: prove the cross-cutting invariants no single plan's tests cover, then confirm on
a physical device the things that cannot be automated — that a real ad renders, that the cap binds,
and that the pause reads as intent rather than as a dead button.

Purpose: three of this phase's load-bearing claims are not decidable on the JVM. RESEARCH
assumption A1 (`ProcessLifecycleOwner` does not dispatch `ON_STOP` for a same-process fullscreen ad
Activity) is the one that matters most — if it is wrong, the D-04 session cap silently never binds
and the failure is invisible in every unit test. STATE.md also carries a standing caution that
Phase 7's manual audit was emulator-only and globally approved rather than itemised; this
checkpoint is itemised on purpose.
</objective>

  <how-to-verify>
    **Build and device.** Install a fresh **debug** build on a **physical device** (not the
    emulator — STATE.md carries a standing caution that Phase 7's audit was emulator-only). Debug
    builds resolve Google's test interstitial unit automatically, so the ad you see is a test ad and
    no real inventory is served. Uninstall first: a fresh install is required because step 2 depends
    on `app_prefs` being empty.

    **Steps 3 and 8 need the device clock.** Settings -> Date & time -> turn OFF automatic date &
    time, then set it manually. **Restore automatic time when you are done.**

    1. Launch the app and accept the consent prompt when it appears.

    2. Play and SOLVE **5 Challenge levels**. **Expect: no ad, no dim, no delay at any point** —
       not after any win, not on any Next-level tap. (D-03: the first session and the first 5
       lifetime levels are both interstitial-free.)

    3. Press Home to background the app. Move the device clock **forward 31 minutes**. Reopen the
       app. (This ends the first session — D-04's 30-minute background boundary.)

    4. Solve **3 more Challenge levels**. On the third win's Results screen, tap **"Next level"**.
       **Expect:** the screen dims for about half a second — no text, no spinner, just the dim —
       and then a full-screen test ad appears over it.

    5. During that dim in step 4, **tap the screen rapidly and press the back button**.
       **Expect: nothing happens.** No button reacts, the back press does nothing, and your taps do
       not land on the ad when it appears. (INT-04 — this is the whole point of the pause.)

    6. Dismiss the ad. **Expect:** the next level loads immediately, the board is fresh, and its
       timer starts from 00:00 **at that moment** — not from a value that ran while the ad was up.
       (ADSF-01.)

    7. Solve one more level and tap "Next level". **Expect: no ad** — the win counter reset to zero
       and the 90-second gate is still closed. (D-01, D-02.)

    8. **The important one — the session cap (RESEARCH assumption A1).** Keep playing in this SAME
       session, without ever backgrounding the app for 30 minutes, until you have seen **4
       interstitials**. Each one needs 3 more wins and at least 90 seconds since the last ad; you
       may move the clock forward a couple of minutes between attempts to clear the 90-second gate
       faster, but **do not background the app for 30 minutes or more**. After the 4th ad, keep
       playing and keep tapping "Next level". **Expect: no 5th ad in this session.**
       **Please do not skip this step.** If a 5th ad appears, `ProcessLifecycleOwner` reported a
       background while the ad Activity was showing, the session counter reset on every ad, and the
       cap never binds — a failure that is invisible to every automated test in this phase.

    9. **Rewarded adjacency (D-15).** Start a level, use hints/undos until you are offered a
       rewarded ad, and watch it. Then win that level and tap "Next level" **within 90 seconds** of
       the rewarded ad. **Expect: no interstitial** — a rewarded ad opens the same 90-second
       cooldown.

    10. **Losses and exits never pay (D-06, INT-03).** After a win, tap **"Replay"**, then **"Home"**
        from a Results screen. On a Classic board, finish with a poor result (1 star) and tap
        **"Catalog"**. On a stuck Challenge board, tap **"Retry"**. **Expect: no ad and no dim on
        any of these four**, and instant navigation with no perceptible delay.

    11. **Survives being killed (INT-02).** Solve one or two levels, then swipe the app away from
        recents and relaunch it **within a couple of minutes**. Solve levels again.
        **Expect:** progress toward the next ad is preserved — you do not get an immediate ad on
        relaunch, and you do not have to start the 3-win count over.

    12. **Judgement call, and it is the one the ROADMAP actually asks for.** Across the whole run:
        did any ad feel resented? Did the half-second dim ever read as "the button didn't work"
        rather than as "something is coming"? Was the forward click sound followed by the dim
        awkward? Say so plainly if any of it grated — the phase's governing sentence is
        "a player barely notices and never resents", and the cadence numbers were chosen against
        the second half of that.

    **Finally: restore automatic date & time on the device.**
  </how-to-verify>
