<objective>
Build the pure-`:domain` deterministic reverse-generation core — the project's #1 risk — and lock its two output-agnostic invariants (solvable-by-construction, byte-stable-on-regeneration) with JVM tests. This plan ships the generator's determinism *mechanism* (portable `stableSeed`, sorted reverse-candidate enumeration, single-threaded synchronous `generate`) plus the data-driven `DifficultyTier` structure. Knob VALUES are playtest-deferred (D-07) and MUST be named constants, never inline literals.

Purpose: Every other Phase-3 deliverable sits on top of this. Solvability is guaranteed by construction (reverse-walk from the solved 1-peg board), and determinism is guaranteed by the seeding/enumeration discipline — asserted here by invariant tests that survive later gate additions.
Output: `GeneratedLevel`, `GeneratorConstants`, `DifficultyTier`, `DifficultyTiers`, `LevelGenerator`, and the solvability + tier-monotonicity tests.
</objective>
