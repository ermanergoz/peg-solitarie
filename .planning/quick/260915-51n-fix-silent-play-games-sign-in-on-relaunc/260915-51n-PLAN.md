<objective>
Fix Phase 11 UAT test 1: a cold relaunch of an opted-in player resolves to SignedOut because
`PlayGamesSdk.initialize` is first called from a coroutine dispatched out of
`MainActivity.onCreate` — i.e. after the only Activity already exists — so the SDK's
`AutomaticGamesAuthenticator`, whose sole trigger is `onActivityCreated` of an Activity created
*after* initialize, never fires its one automatic attempt, and `isAuthenticated()` (which only
reports that authenticator's cached state) answers not-authenticated.

Purpose: restore real silent sign-in and the stock "Welcome back" popup on relaunch (PGS-01)
**without** weakening D-07/D-17 — a player who never opted in must still never be touched by the
SDK, which is why `PlayGamesInitProvider` stays removed and initialize stays behind an opt-in gate.

Output: a new `GamesService.initializeIfOptedIn()` seam method called from `App.onCreate()`,
its SDK-backed implementation, stub/fake implementations, tests, refreshed KDoc/manifest comments,
and a recorded emulator verification in `11-HUMAN-UAT.md`.
