## Decisions Made

- **Safe-zone scale 0.69** (from the plan). Real extent after scaling is 152.6 units =
  32.2dp radius, comfortably inside the 33dp safe radius.
- **Monochrome variant: all 33 holes**, hole radius 15 (centre hole 20) so roughly 3.8dp
  of webbing survives a flat tint at 48dp.
- **One icon resource for both `icon` and `roundIcon`.**
- **Legacy foreground shared, not re-authored.**
- **Icon palette in its own resource file** so it is greppable as a unit and cannot be
  mistaken for theme tokens.

## Deliberate Fidelity Losses (recorded per success criterion 8)

1. **No blurred drop shadows.** VectorDrawable has no blur primitive, so the slab's
   `drop-shadow(0 18px 20px rgba(46,16,101,0.45))` and each peg's
   `0 6px 10px rgba(46,16,101,0.35)` are dropped. Launchers add their own elevation
   shadow, and the pegs' dark `#BE185D` gradient rim already reads as grounding. A
   hard-edged offset duplicate — the only shadow a vector could draw — would look like a
   rendering bug at 48dp.
2. **Specular ellipses are not rotated.** The design applies `rotate(-25deg)` to a 12x8
   highlight; at final size that ellipse is ~1.7dp wide, where the rotation is invisible,
   and rotating it would cost 32 extra `<group>` wrappers.
3. **Occupied-cell dimples are flat, not inset-shaded.** Only their lower crescent shows
   under the peg. The centre hole — the cell the whole design is about — keeps its
   two-tone inset treatment (radius-20 shadow ring, radius-17 inner disc dropped 3 units).
4. **Monochrome hole radius is 15, not 20** (centre hole stays 20), to preserve legible
   webbing under a flat tint.

### Observation (not auto-fixed): peg vertical offset differs from the design canvas by 2 units

- **Found during:** Task 2, while sanity-checking the plan's numbers against
  `Main.dc.html` as the plan's `<geometry_reference>` invites.
- **Detail:** The approved design places each peg at `cy - 4` (dimple div `top:68` →
  centre y 88; peg div `top:62`, 44px → centre y 84) and its specular at
  `(px - 6, py - 11)`. The plan specifies `py = cy - 6` with the same specular formula,
  a 2-unit difference on a 512-unit canvas.
- **Action:** Followed the plan verbatim (`py = cy - 6`), per the executor constraint.
  The plan's worked example (`peg M178,82`, gradient centre `193.4/73.2`, specular
  `M188,71`) is reproduced exactly by the generator, so the output matches what the plan
  asked for.
- **Impact:** 2 design units at 0.69 scale is 0.29dp on the 108dp canvas, ~0.13dp at a
  48dp render — below a pixel on any density. The visible consequence is that the dimple
  crescent under each peg is 4 units tall rather than 2, i.e. very slightly more visible
  seating shadow. Flag it at the checkpoint only if the pegs read as sitting too high.

**Total deviations:** 0 auto-fixed. **Impact on plan:** none; no scope creep.
